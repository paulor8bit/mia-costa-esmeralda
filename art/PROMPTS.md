## Mia

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production game sprite sheet for a LÖVE 2D platformer. Create ONE square PNG transparent sprite atlas with EXACTLY 4 columns and 4 rows, 16 separate full-body sprites, evenly spaced identical square cells, no labels, no grid lines. Character Mia is an extremely cute fluffy cream and peach female kitten, big shiny teal eyes, tiny pink nose, little pink bow on her left ear, pink scarf, mint running shoes, fluffy curved tail. Gorgeous polished 16-bit SNES-era pixel art with deliberate crisp pixel clusters, rich 5-tone shading, warm highlights, dark plum selective outlines, expressive face, detailed soft fur represented by pixels. Consistent design and size in every cell, facing right in side/three-quarter platformer view. Character fully within each cell with generous transparent margins, feet same baseline around 80% of cell, head top 12%. Row 1: four idle frames gently breathing, one blink. Row 2: four distinct running cycle poses, legs and arms alternate with obvious motion, tail bouncing. Row 3: jump ascending, jump apex, falling pose, tucked rolling curled-up ball. Row 4: spinning ball, cheerful victory raised paw, waving paw, cute crouch. Pure genuine alpha background, no ground, no shadows outside character, no scenery. This must look substantially more beautiful and detailed than primitive 8-bit graphics. No text or watermark.

## Costa

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: seamless horizontal parallax background for a beautiful 16-bit side-scrolling platform game. Wide landscape 16:9 image, no characters, no text, no foreground ground or platforms. Rich polished SNES-era pixel art, crisp intentional square pixels and luxurious layered color ramps, not crude 8-bit, not vector. Bright idyllic emerald tropical coastline: soft turquoise sky, luminous fluffy ivory clouds across upper half, distant hazy violet coastal mountains, emerald island cliffs with cascading waterfalls at middle distance, sparkling azure sea across bottom third, tiny coral flowers and lush foliage silhouettes only near lower edges. Sunny charming magical vacation mood, visually rich but quiet enough behind game sprites. Camera side view. Horizon at 65% height. Perfect left/right seamless wrapping, both edges predominantly matching sky and sea, avoid prominent unique objects at edges. Large readable clusters, detailed dithering on cliffs and water, saturated turquoise emerald pink and warm cream palette, beautiful professional 1990s 16-bit game art. No HUD no lettering no checkerboard.

One image with EXACTLY 4 equal columns by 2 equal rows, eight isolated assets, each fully contained in its own equal square cell with generous alpha margins, no labels or grid. Polished SNES 16-bit pixel art with crisp pixel clusters, rich detailed multi-tone shading, dark plum selective outlines, turquoise emerald coral pink and warm golden cream colors. Upper row left to right: 1 lush tropical palm tree with curved textured trunk and abundant beautifully shaded emerald fronds, 2 cute enemy mechanical crab with coral armor teal chrome claws articulated legs and glowing eyes, 3 pink red metal coiled bounce spring on gold footing, 4 large round mechanical guardian robot teal steel armor gold rivets pink glowing eyes and chunky legs. Bottom row left to right: 1 solid square seamless repeating tile of richly textured warm ochre sandstone soil with embedded small rounded stones, roots and subtle fossils, absolutely no grass or border, filling its own entire cell edge-to-edge, 2 small lush flowering grass clump with pink hibiscus flowers and tiny white daisies, 3 gleaming golden collectible ring with hollow transparent center and four-point sparkle upper right, 4 checkpoint flag on slender golden pole with pink pennant bearing a small cream paw emblem and grassy foot. Side view suitable for a platform game. Assets centered horizontally and bottoms consistently around 92% cell height. True alpha transparent background apart from the square terrain tile, no scenery, no letters no watermark. These are beautiful detailed 16-bit assets not primitive geometric icons.
